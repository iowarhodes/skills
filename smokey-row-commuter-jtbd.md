# Smokey Row Morning Commuter — Jobs to Be Done

## Core Audience
The regular commuter who swings by Smokey Row on the way to work — it's a habitual, routine stop baked into their daily drive, not a one-off treat.

## Job Statement
When I'm on my morning commute to work, but drive-thru lines take too long and I can't risk being late, help me get caffeinated and ready for the day, so I still make it to my desk on time with a drink that genuinely perks me up.

## Top Desired Outcomes
- Still make it to the desk on time despite stopping
- Get a drink that's genuinely enjoyable, not just functional caffeine
- Feel perked up and ready to tackle the day

## What They're Hiring / Firing Today
- Hiring: Brewing at home (Nespresso), other drive-thru chains, gas station coffee
- Firing: Homemade chai lattes — abandoned because they don't taste the same as the café version

## What's Pulling Them Back to the Status Quo
Home brewing with a Nespresso is more convenient (and cheaper) than the stop — but Smokey Row still feels like a treat worth making time for.

## Job Map
Stage-by-stage job statements for the same commuter, scoped to each slice of the "get coffee on the way to work" process.

- **Define:** When I'm getting ready to leave for work, but I don't know yet if today's the kind of morning I have time to detour, help me decide fast whether the stop is worth the risk, so I'm not still debating it once I'm driving.
- **Locate:** When I'm approaching the road near Smokey Row, but I can't tell how long the line is until I've already committed to the turn, help me get a read on the wait beforehand, so I'm not gambling my schedule on a blind guess.
- **Prepare:** When I'm pulling into the lot or lane, but I haven't settled on an order yet, help me know what I'm getting before I reach the window, so I don't stall the line behind me.
- **Confirm:** When I'm about to get in line, but I'm not sure I actually have the time or a card handy, help me quickly check I can pull this off, so I don't commit and then have to bail mid-line.
- **Execute:** When I'm at the window, but I want the exchange over fast, help me order and pay without friction, so I get my drink and get moving.
- **Monitor:** When I'm sitting in line, but I don't have a clear read on how much time is passing, help me keep a loose eye on the clock, so I know if I need to bail before it's too late.
- **Modify:** When the line runs longer than expected, but I'm already committed, help me decide fast whether to wait it out, simplify my order, or peel off, so I still protect my arrival time.
- **Conclude:** When I've got my drink, but I still have to get back on the road and into the workday, help me wrap the stop up cleanly, so the treat doesn't turn into the reason I'm flustered at my desk.
